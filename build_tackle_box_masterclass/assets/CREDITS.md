# Third-party assets

No 3D models, textures or sound samples from other sources are used: the tackle box, every lure, hook,
the baitfish, the studio and water backgrounds are modelled and shaded by tackle_box_masterclass.py, and
the music and effects are synthesised by tackle_box_audio.py.

## voiceover/ : generated speech
Text to speech generated with Higgsfield (text2speech_v2) from the script in voiceover/VO_SCRIPT.txt, in the
user's Higgsfield account. Rights follow Higgsfield's terms of service for content generated by the
account holder (and those of the speech engine it routes to); check them before commercial use.

## fonts/ : SIL Open Font License 1.1
Inter (Bold, SemiBold, Medium) and Playfair Display. The Playfair Display SemiBold, Medium, SemiBold
Italic and Bold Italic files are static instances made with fontTools from the official variable fonts
(github.com/google/fonts, ofl/playfairdisplay). See fonts/OFL-*.txt.
