Bluefin record masterclass: voiceover script (five clips, about 50 seconds in total)
Delivery: intense, fast paced, clean cadence, authoritative. Save the clips as vo1.mp3 to vo5.mp3.

vo1 (the hook)
In 1979, Ken Fraser hooked a 1,496 pound bluefin tuna off Nova Scotia. A record that hasn't been touched in over 40 years.

vo2 (the gear)
To target fish this size, average gear will snap in seconds. You need 130 pound class monofilament, spooled on a massive fourteen aught reel, trolling fresh mackerel or herring deep behind the boat.

vo3 (the thermal strike zone)
Giant bluefin don't just swim anywhere; they track temperature breaks. Tagging studies show their dives follow the thermocline, the boundary between warm surface water and the cold water below. And they can dive past three thousand feet.

When a half ton fish hits, the angler doesn't fight alone. The captain must back the boat down hard to keep line on the spool, while the angler holds heavy drag.
