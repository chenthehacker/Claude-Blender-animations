## khronos/CesiumMan.glb : Cesium Man, CC BY 4.0
Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CesiumMan
(commit edc7c9e67c639d230715049ee31f9a96a6babbbe; copied from build_flathead_catfish_masterclass/assets). Copyright
Cesium, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International; the license excludes the Cesium logo
and trademarks (see khronos/LICENSE_CesiumMan.md). Changes: the head (which carries the Cesium logo) is
deleted and replaced with a modelled head, beard, cap and sunglasses; the original texture is not used (the
body is recoloured procedurally as orange bib trousers, a grey top and white deck boots); the walk animation is
removed; the body is seated in the fighting chair with inverse kinematics on the arms (to the rod) and legs (to the
footrest); the model is scaled from 1.45 m to 1.8 m.

## voiceover/ : generated speech
The voiceover clips (vo1 to vo5) are generated with ElevenLabs text to speech, voice "Mark - Natural
Conversations", from the script in voiceover/VO_SCRIPT.txt: vo1 and vo5 by the user in their ElevenLabs account and
uploaded to this project, vo2 to vo4 through the user's ElevenLabs connector. Rights follow ElevenLabs' terms of
service for the account holder (check whether your plan allows commercial use).

Everything else (the sea, sky and swells, the bluefin, mackerel and baitfish, the sportfisher, fighting chair, rod
and reel, the water column diagram, plankton glow, spray, overlays) is generated by bluefin_record_masterclass.py; the
music and sound effects are synthesised by bluefin_audio.py.
