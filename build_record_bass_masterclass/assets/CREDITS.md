## khronos/CesiumMan.glb : Cesium Man, CC BY 4.0
Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CesiumMan
(commit edc7c9e67c639d230715049ee31f9a96a6babbbe; copied from build_sockeye_masterclass/assets). Copyright
Cesium, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International; the license excludes the Cesium logo
and trademarks (see khronos/LICENSE_CesiumMan.md). Changes: the head (which carries the Cesium logo) is
deleted and replaced with a modelled head, beard, cap and sunglasses; the original texture is not used (the
body is recoloured procedurally as a shirt, trousers and boots); the walk animation is removed, the arms are
posed with inverse kinematics and the body leans back against the rod; the model is scaled from 1.45 m to 1.8 m.

## voiceover/ : generated speech
The voiceover clips (vo1 to vo5) are generated with Higgsfield text to speech (voice "Alexey") by the user
from the script in voiceover/VO_SCRIPT.txt and uploaded to this project. Rights follow Higgsfield's terms of
service for content generated by the account holder.

Everything else (reservoir bed, flooded oak, standing timber, boulders, the largemouth bass, trout, shad,
bluegill, the jointed swimbait, the boat, rod, water, sky, sun shafts, plankton, overlays) is generated by
record_bass_masterclass.py; the music and sound effects are synthesised by record_bass_audio.py.
