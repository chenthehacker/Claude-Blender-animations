## higgsfield/sockeye_tripo.glb : AI generated model (Higgsfield, Tripo text to 3D)
Generated on 15 October 2026 for this project from the user's Higgsfield account (model "tripo_3d", text
prompt describing a spawning male sockeye; job f71b2b70-f418-49ed-a525-12e42b47c3d0) and supplied by the
user. Rights follow Higgsfield's terms of service for content generated by the account holder; check them
before commercial use. The scene rotates, rescales and recentres the mesh, keeps its colour, normal and
ORM textures, raises the clear coat and subsurface weight, and bends it with a swimming deformation.

## khronos/CesiumMan.glb : Cesium Man, CC BY 4.0
Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CesiumMan
(commit edc7c9e67c639d230715049ee31f9a96a6babbbe). Copyright Cesium, licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International; the license excludes the Cesium logo and trademarks (see
khronos/LICENSE_CesiumMan.md). Changes: the head (which carries the Cesium logo) is deleted and replaced
with a modelled head, beard, cap and sunglasses; the original texture is not used (the body is recoloured
procedurally as chest waders, boots and a jacket); the walk animation is removed and the arms are posed
with inverse kinematics; the model is scaled from 1.45 m to 1.8 m.

## fonts/ : SIL Open Font License 1.1
Inter (Bold, SemiBold, Medium) and Playfair Display. The Playfair Display SemiBold, Medium, SemiBold
Italic and Bold Italic files are static instances made with fontTools from the official variable fonts
(github.com/google/fonts, ofl/playfairdisplay). See fonts/OFL-*.txt.

generated by sockeye_masterclass.py; the soundtrack is synthesised by sockeye_audio.py.
