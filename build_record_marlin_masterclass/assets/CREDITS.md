# Third-party assets

No third-party 3D models are used in this scene, so no CC BY attribution is required for models.

## fonts/ : SIL Open Font License 1.1
Inter (Bold, SemiBold, Medium) and Playfair Display (static instances made with fontTools from the official
variable fonts, github.com/google/fonts, ofl/playfairdisplay). See fonts/OFL-*.txt.

## voiceover/ : generated speech
The voiceover clips (vo1 to vo5) are generated with ElevenLabs text to speech, voice "Mark, Natural
Conversations", from the script in voiceover/VO_SCRIPT.txt: vo1 to vo4 through the user's ElevenLabs connector,
vo5 by the user in their ElevenLabs account and uploaded to this project. Rights follow ElevenLabs' terms of service
for the account holder (check whether your plan allows commercial use).

Everything else (the sea and sky, the sportfisher, rods, reel and lures, the blue marlin, skipjack and baitfish,
the drop off diagram, bubbles, spray and overlays) is generated by record_marlin_masterclass.py; the music and sound
effects are synthesised by record_marlin_audio.py.
