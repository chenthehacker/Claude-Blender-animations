# Third-party assets

No third-party 3D models are used in this scene, so no CC BY attribution is required for models.

## fonts/ : SIL Open Font License 1.1
Inter (Bold, SemiBold, Medium) and Playfair Display (static instances made with fontTools from the official
variable fonts, github.com/google/fonts, ofl/playfairdisplay). See fonts/OFL-*.txt.

## voiceover/ : generated speech
The voiceover clips (vo1 to vo5) are generated with ElevenLabs text to speech, voice "Mark - Natural
Conversations", from the script in voiceover/VO_SCRIPT.txt: vo1 and vo2 through the user's ElevenLabs connector,
vo3 to vo5 by the user in their ElevenLabs account and uploaded to this project. Rights follow ElevenLabs' terms of
service for the account holder (check whether your plan allows commercial use).

Everything else (the harbour, pier, lamp and light cone, the squid, the egi jig, the baitfish, plankton sparks,
overlays) is generated by night_squid_masterclass.py; the music and sound effects are synthesised by
night_squid_audio.py.
