# Third-party assets

The files in this folder are the downloads, unmodified (renamed only), used under free licenses.
The scene changes them when it builds (CC BY 4.0 asks you to say so): the rock pack is split into
single rocks, rescaled, and the open undersides are filled; the textures are tinted, blended and
darkened in shaders; the fish get a swimming deformation.
If you publish a video made with them, credit the CC BY 4.0 assets, for example in the description:
"Rock, seabed, sand, seagrass, water and grey snapper assets (modified): Babylon.js Assets, CC BY 4.0,
https://github.com/BabylonJS/Assets. Barramundi fish: Microsoft, Khronos glTF Sample Assets (CC0)."
