# Third-party assets

No third-party 3D models are used in this scene, so no CC BY attribution is required for models.

## fonts/ : SIL Open Font License 1.1
Inter (Bold, SemiBold, Medium) and Playfair Display (static instances made with fontTools from the official
variable fonts, github.com/google/fonts, ofl/playfairdisplay). See fonts/OFL-*.txt.

## voiceover/ : generated speech
The voiceover clips (vo1 to vo5) are generated with ElevenLabs text to speech, voice "Mark, Natural
Conversations", model eleven_v4, from the script in voiceover/VO_SCRIPT.txt: vo1, vo2, vo3 and vo5 through the
user's ElevenLabs connector, vo4 by the user in their own ElevenLabs account (same voice). Rights follow ElevenLabs'
terms of service for the account holder (check whether your plan allows commercial use).

Everything else (the lake bed, boulders and the cut away cross section, the cisco style baitfish, the rainbow trout,
the jointed crankbait, line and leader, the boat, rod and spinning reel, silt, bubbles, light shafts and overlays) is
generated by record_trout_masterclass.py; the music and sound effects are synthesised by record_trout_audio.py.
