# Third-party assets

No third-party 3D models are used in this scene, so no CC BY attribution is required for models.

## fonts/ : SIL Open Font License 1.1
Inter (Bold, SemiBold, Medium) and Playfair Display (static instances made with fontTools from the official
variable fonts, github.com/google/fonts, ofl/playfairdisplay). See fonts/OFL-*.txt.

## voiceover/ : generated speech
The voiceover clips (vo1 to vo5) are generated with ElevenLabs text to speech, voice "Mark, Natural
Conversations", model eleven_v4, through the user's ElevenLabs connector, from the script in
voiceover/VO_SCRIPT.txt. Rights follow ElevenLabs' terms of service for the account holder (check whether your plan
allows commercial use).

Everything else (the sea, the shipwreck and its cross section, sand, crabs, baitfish, the goliath grouper, the rig,
the boat, rod and reel, silt, bubbles, light shafts and overlays) is generated by record_goliath_masterclass.py; the
music and sound effects are synthesised by record_goliath_audio.py.
